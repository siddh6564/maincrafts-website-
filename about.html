Our Mission

Crafting digital products that make a difference

MainCrafts was founded in 2019 by a group of engineers and designers who believed that too many great ideas were being let down by mediocre execution. We set out to fix that.

Today we are a 40-person team spanning design, engineering, and strategy. We've shipped over 200 products for startups, scale-ups, and enterprise clients across 15+ countries.

Our process is simple: understand deeply, design boldly, build precisely. We don't just deliver code — we deliver outcomes.
